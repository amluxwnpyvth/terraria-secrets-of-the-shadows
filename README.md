## Overview

Secrets of the Shadows expands Terraria's underground Corruption and Shadow biomes with new enemy variants, shadow-themed boss encounters with multi-phase attack scripts, dark lore items that hint at the world's hidden history, and a complete shadow-biome equipment tier with unique weapon mechanics centered around void energy and shadow matter.

Whether you're a newcomer just getting started or a seasoned pro looking for advanced tools, **Terraria Secrets of the Shadows** delivers the features you need in a clean, intuitive package.
